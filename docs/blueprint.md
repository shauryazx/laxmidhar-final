# **App Name**: Laxmidhar College Connect

## Core Features:

- About Us & History Display: Showcase the college's mission, vision, and detailed history to new visitors.
- Programs Offered Listing: Provide comprehensive information on all academic programs, including Diploma courses in Mechanical, Electrical, and Civil Engineering with their respective seat capacities as per provided details.
- Admissions Details Portal: Offer clear, step-by-step guidance on the admission process, eligibility criteria, and important dates.
- News & Events Feed: Display the latest college news, upcoming events, announcements, and important updates in an easily navigable format.
- Contact & Location Information: Provide essential contact details, a map showing the college location (1N-3 MIA RIICO, Near IET College, Mungaska, Alwar-301001, Rajasthan), and inquiry forms.
- AI-Powered FAQ Tool: An interactive AI tool that can answer common questions regarding admissions, programs, and general college information using natural language processing.
- HODs Desk: Dedicated section featuring messages, insights, and updates directly from the Heads of Departments, Director Nirma Choudhary, and Principal Shahkool Khan. This page also displays profiles and contact information for all key administrative roles including Chairman Mahendra Singh Choudhary, Director, Principal, and HODs for Civil (Nikita Jagid), Mechanical (Jya Seini), and Electrical Engineering (Sunil Mahiwal), providing a personal touch and academic guidance.
- Mandatory Disclosure AICTE: A dedicated page displaying all mandatory disclosures and information required by AICTE, ensuring transparency and compliance.
- Chairman's Desk: A dedicated section featuring messages, insights, and strategic updates directly from Chairman Mahendra Singh Choudhary, providing a personal perspective on the college's vision and direction.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: A deep, authoritative Ocean Blue (#004D80), symbolizing wisdom, professionalism, and stability, inspired by rich blue ink on paper.
- Accent color: A sophisticated Vibrant Gold (#F2A200), providing an attractive contrast and symbolizing excellence, achievement, and prestige.
- Background color: A clean, spacious Off-White (#F8F8F8), offering a bright canvas for content, reminiscent of clean paper.
- Secondary neutral color: A calming Cool Grey Blue (#607D8B), used for borders, subtle dividers, and secondary information, complementing the primary blue.
- Text color: A highly readable Dark Slate Grey (#263238), ensuring optimal contrast and a professional appearance across all textual elements.
- Headline and body text font: 'Inter', a versatile sans-serif font known for its clarity and readability across various screen sizes, ensuring a modern and objective look suitable for an informative website.
- Use a set of clean, minimalist line icons that align with modern web design principles and clearly communicate information without clutter.
- Employ a responsive grid-based layout for optimal viewing across devices, with ample whitespace to ensure a professional, uncluttered, and easy-to-digest content presentation.
- Incorporate subtle, tasteful micro-interactions and smooth transitions on hover effects and navigation, providing a polished and engaging user experience without being distracting.
